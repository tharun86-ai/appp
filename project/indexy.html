📖 English Grammar

Present Simple

Used for habits, routines and facts.

I work every day.
She works every day.

Present Continuous

Used for actions happening now.

I am studying English.
She is reading.

Past Simple

Used for completed actions in the past.

I visited London yesterday.
He played football.

Future Simple

Used for future decisions and predictions.

I will call you tomorrow.

Articles: A / An / The

Use a before consonant sounds and an before vowel sounds.

a book
an apple
the sun

Prepositions

Words showing relationships between things.

The book is on the table.
She is in the room.

Comparatives

Used to compare two things.

John is taller than Mike.

Modal Verbs

Can, could, should, must and may.

You should study every day.
